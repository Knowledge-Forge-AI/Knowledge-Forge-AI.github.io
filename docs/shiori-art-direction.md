# Shiori art direction

Shiori is an original adult high elf created as Agentic Praxis Grimoire’s mascot.
She has fair skin, dark purple hair, dark blue eyes, a feminine curving silhouette
and a height of about 5 feet 4 inches. Purple, blue and silver express her
mysterious, intelligent and otherworldly scholarly character. She is fascinated
by grimoires and appears fully clothed, reading an ornate magical book.

The image was generated with the built-in image tool using an original fantasy
anime character brief. The illustration is not intended to depict a named
existing character. `public/apgr/shiori.webp` retains the complete character and
transparency. Its SHA-256 is
`1232c9de2fe441aae6586937aad627c591f7d3db21c34145140342b2d6c920b8`.
The master, selected website references and full prompts are retained in the
owner’s private artwork archive; no private archive or local paths are published.

See BRAND-ASSETS.md and NOTICE for the artwork exception. The website’s software
and editorial content retain the repository’s existing licensing terms.
