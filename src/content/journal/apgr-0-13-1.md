A package's compatibility label needs to describe the executable it contains. APGR 0.13.1 corrects the Apple Silicon wheel from a macOS 11 tag to macOS 12 or later, matching the declared minimum observed in the earlier binary.

The release strengthens the packaging seams so a Mach-O minimum and wheel tag cannot silently disagree. Linux wheel targets remain unchanged, and the same forty-six-skill corpus and dispatcher continue from 0.13.0. Previously published assets keep their original identities; a correction receives its own version.

The tagged notes were prepared before publication and retain that candidate wording. Use the published release record and attestations for release identity. Its separate CI-tool risk dispositions are not a blanket claim that every dependency is vulnerability-free.
