The catalog grows from nineteen to twenty-eight skills, with a dedicated ChatGPT-manager routing surface and nine new provisional capabilities. Management, planning, worker assignments, execution and acceptance remain distinct responsibilities.

The release also strengthens the machinery around the skills: Python-first operational reports, a pytest-based test architecture and version-aware release validation. That infrastructure makes a recorded result easier to inspect and a public candidate easier to reproduce.

All nine additions remain provisional. Publication makes a capability available for inspection; it does not by itself justify a maturity promotion or authorize an agent to dispatch work.
