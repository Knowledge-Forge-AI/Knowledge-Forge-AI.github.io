GoMock, Vitest, JSX, React, MDX and Astro join the public library, bringing it to thirty-nine skills. Each profile addresses a particular seam: a framework question should not silently become a JavaScript, test-runner or host-runtime decision.

Project-selected projection remains authoritative. This version does not introduce automatic or advisory profile selection, and cross-profile work chooses the narrowest relevant owners rather than an aggregate stack owner.

All six additions are provisional. Qualification exercised real web and Go compositions and retained an honest locked-install stop in the Terminal Nova consumer instead of bypassing its lockfile. A failed consumer prerequisite is evidence to preserve, not a reason to broaden a success claim.
