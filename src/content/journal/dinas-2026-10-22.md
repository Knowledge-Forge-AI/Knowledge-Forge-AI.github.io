DINAS—Darwin Is Not a Snowflake—grew from the work of making a workstation explicit: its packages, configuration, source selection, qualification and attended deployment. When shell wrappers, Nix modules and application adapters interact, understanding the system becomes a problem of relationships rather than a list of files.

That need was the original inspiration for RepoMap. A machine configuration worth maintaining also needed a map of how its pieces connect. RepoMap carries that concern into deterministic, evidence-linked knowledge graphs for polyglot repositories.

The v0.0.1 public Core preview is a curated source snapshot with example identities and a modular nix-darwin operating model. The composition preview preserves adapter registrations and ownership contracts, but some adapters still require authorized access; an unauthenticated clone cannot build the entire composition.

These are source previews, not a minimal unattended installer, a security certification or a host activation. Source promotion, qualification and a privileged workstation switch remain separate operations.
