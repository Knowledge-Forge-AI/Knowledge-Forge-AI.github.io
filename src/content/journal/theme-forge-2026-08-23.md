An asset pipeline becomes more interesting when upstream changes. Stellar Burst 0.2.0 adds incremental reconciliation with paired provenance, deterministic bundle exchange and comparisons across semantic baselines.

Reconciliation is read-only by default. Conflicts, renames and removals need explicit decisions rather than silent overwrite or deletion. Portable bundles preserve asset identities and hashes without carrying private installation destinations into the exchange.

Canonical formatting, relocatable receipts and offline preview galleries round out the release. The focus is the full lifecycle: not only producing a vector once, but understanding how that vector changed and whether an installed copy still matches its source.
