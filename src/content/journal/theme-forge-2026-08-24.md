The new pre-import analysis surface classifies archive entries before they enter a project. A directly importable asset, an asset needing normalization, an unsupported construct and an unsafe input are different outcomes, and the report keeps them different.

Schema 2 adds typed primitive shapes and transforms, structured accessibility modes, local definitions and references, and deterministic migration and reconciliation. Offline scriptless galleries give the output a visual review surface.

Schema 1 compatibility stays frozen. A richer model does not justify silently changing the meaning of an existing project, and normalization is an explicit operation rather than an invisible repair.
