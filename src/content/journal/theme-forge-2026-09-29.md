This release day advances three independently versioned parts of Theme Forge: Stellar Burst 0.6.1, Stellar Loom 0.4.0 and Solar Sail 0.2.1. The published components have their own source and distribution identities.

That independence is useful, but it creates a distinction worth making explicit: publishing a compiler patch does not replace the compiler already embedded in a desktop application. A bundled workbench needs its own qualified successor before it can claim the new input.

The published records preserve the frozen artifacts and their producer provenance. For exact changes, use each version's source and changelog rather than assuming that all family products advance together or share one version number.
