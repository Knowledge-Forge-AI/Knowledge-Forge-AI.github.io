Nebular 0.7.0 corrects plan-authority notifications across initial mount and StrictMode replay, strengthens native graphics-provider authentication and lifecycle diagnostics, and prepares gallery themes from authenticated locked inputs.

The native archives and npm-format packages cover Apple Silicon, Linux ARM64 and Linux AMD64. They were compiled and exercised on their matching public GitHub Actions environments, with Debian Bookworm used for Linux qualification.

The important connection is between a source release and a real packaged consumer. Source tests, native builds and application scenarios answer different questions; the release carries their identities and hashes so those answers can be traced to the artifacts actually distributed.
