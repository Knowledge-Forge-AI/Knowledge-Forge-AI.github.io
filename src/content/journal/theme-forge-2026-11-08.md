Nebular 0.8.0 brings paired-v2 profile authoring into the workbench. A brief can lead to Solar Sail and Stellar Loom previews, candidate-packet exchange, review, and drafts that save and reopen.

Application appearances now offer six families in light and dark, System/Light/Dark behavior, independent family preferences for the two modes, and a reset to bundled defaults. These are application settings rather than a static screenshot of a proposed interface.

The Apple Silicon target moves to macOS 13.3 or later. Linux AMD64 and ARM64 use the system WebKitGTK desktop runtime. The release links its source and native qualification, including persistence/reopen and appearance cases; the macOS developer application is not represented as Developer ID signed or notarized.
